2.2 Provenance & Checkpoint Lineage
Tracing the commit history, published articles, and documentation from the upstream project (shubham0204/OnDevice-Face-Recognition-Android and shubham0204/FaceRecognition_With_FaceNet_Android):

Step 1 (Shubham Panchal's Conversion): Shubham Panchal documented sourcing the Keras .h5 model files directly from the public GitHub repository nyoki-mtl/keras-facenet and converting them with tf.lite.TFLiteConverter.
Step 2 (nyoki-mtl/keras-facenet Origin): nyoki-mtl ported the weights directly from David Sandberg's repository (davidsandberg/facenet), the widely adopted open-source TensorFlow implementation of Google's FaceNet paper (Schroff et al., CVPR 2015).
Step 3 (The Specific Checkpoints):
128-d model: Sandberg checkpoint 20170512-110547 (Inception-ResNet-v1 trained on CASIA-WebFace).
512-d model: Sandberg checkpoint 20180402-114759 or 20180408-102900 (Inception-ResNet-v1 trained on VGGFace2).
2.3 Training Dataset Restrictions (The Critical Legal Conflict)
While both David Sandberg's repository and nyoki-mtl's repository carry the permissive MIT License, the MIT License covers only their Python code, not the proprietary or restricted training weights:

CASIA-WebFace (128-d model):
Source: Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA).
Terms: The CASIA-WebFace dataset agreement explicitly restricts usage to academic research and non-commercial purposes only. Commercial use, inclusion in commercial products, and commercial distribution of models derived from it are strictly prohibited.
VGGFace2 (512-d model):
Source: Visual Geometry Group (VGG), University of Oxford (funded in part by IARPA/ODNI).
Terms: The VGGFace2 license agreement states: "The dataset is available for non-commercial research purposes only." Commercial exploitation or redistribution of models trained on VGGFace2 is strictly prohibited.
Patent Exposure:
Google holds US Patent 9,805,274 ("Biometric facial recognition using deep neural network architectures and triplet loss").
2.4 Conclusion for FaceNet
CAUTION

P0 Commercial Blocker Confirmed.
Neither facenet.tflite nor facenet_512.tflite can be legally shipped in a commercial product. The underlying training data agreements for both CASIA-WebFace and VGGFace2 explicitly forbid commercial exploitation. In MODEL_LICENSES, their status must remain "VERIFY MANUALLY / UNVERIFIED — P0 blocker" until replaced.

FaceNet Replacement Path Before Commercial Launch:
To survive legal due diligence, EduVision will need to substitute the FaceNet embedding weights with:
A commercially cleared model like MobileFaceNet trained on synthetic datasets or datasets with commercial rights (e.g. Glint360k under commercial agreement), or
An on-device face recognition SDK with cleared vendor licensing.
